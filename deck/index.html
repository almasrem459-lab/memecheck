01 · The problem

Most meme coins don’t survive the day.

i.

Most pump.fun meme coins die within hours.

Launching takes a minute, so serial launchers and coordinated wallets recycle the same playbook.

ii.

The warning signs are on-chain — but scattered.

Dev history, holder clusters, snipers, liquidity and contract flags live across ~5 different tools.

iii.

Decisions happen in minutes, often on a phone.

Nobody cross-checks five dashboards on a 6-inch screen before a swap.
