Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-21. Plain language. MemeCheckShield is a free, open-source token safety checker at memecheck.pages.dev.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no wallet connection required to scan, no private keys or seed phrases — ever. We will never ask for them.
  • Token addresses you scan are sent to public data sources (DexScreener, RugCheck, GoPlus, Honeypot.is, GeckoTerminal, pump.fun, Solana RPC) to build the result; they are not linked to you.

What we store

  • Pro waitlist (/pro): the email or Telegram handle and optional note you type, used only to tell you when Pro launches. Ask us to delete it any time via the feedback box.
  • Feedback box and wallet-label disputes: your message and optional contact, plus a salted hash of your IP used only for rate limiting (3 posts per hour). No raw IP is stored.
  • Community chat (/chat): messages and nickname you post, plus a salted IP hash for rate limits and bans. Messages are public.
  • Shared scan cache: scan results (token data only, no personal data) are cached server-side to save rate limits.
  • Your browser: language, watchlist and recent scans are kept in your own browser (localStorage); we do not receive them.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page views and anonymous events (for example “scan done”, risk level shown, share clicks). Google may set cookies and process your IP address to produce aggregate statistics; see Google’s privacy policy. You can block it with any content blocker or Google’s opt-out add-on; the site works the same without it.

Infrastructure

The site runs on Cloudflare Pages, Workers, KV and Durable Objects; Cloudflare processes request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the service. Abuse-monitoring logs keep only a short hashed IP.

Your choices

To delete waitlist, feedback or chat data, send a request through the feedback box below with the contact you used. We do not sell personal data.
